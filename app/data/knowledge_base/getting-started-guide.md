# Getting Started with Shakers

## Welcome to Shakers!

Welcome to Shakers, the premier platform connecting businesses with skilled freelance professionals. This guide will walk you through the essential steps to get started, whether you're joining as a client looking to hire talent or as a freelancer offering your services.

## For Clients: Finding and Hiring Talent

### 1. Create Your Account

Getting started on Shakers is simple:

1. Visit [shakers.example.com](https://shakers.example.com)
2. Click "Sign Up" in the top right corner
3. Select "Join as a Client"
4. Complete your profile with:
   - Basic contact information
   - Company details
   - Industry and business type
   - Payment information (securely stored)

### 2. Post Your First Project

Once your account is ready, you can create your first project:

1. Click "Post a Project" from your dashboard
2. Fill in the project details:
   - Clear, descriptive title
   - Detailed project description
   - Required skills and experience
   - Project timeline
   - Budget range and payment type (hourly or fixed-price)
3. Add any relevant attachments or references
4. Review and publish your project

### 3. Review Proposals

After publishing your project, you'll begin receiving proposals from qualified freelancers:

1. Review each proposal carefully, evaluating:
   - Freelancer's experience and portfolio
   - Cover letter and project approach
   - Pricing and timeline
   - Reviews from previous clients
2. Use filters to sort proposals by rating, price, or skills
3. Create a shortlist of promising candidates

### 4. Interview and Select

Before making your final decision:

1. Message freelancers through our secure platform
2. Schedule video interviews using our integrated conferencing tool
3. Request additional work samples if needed
4. Discuss project specifics and clarify expectations

### 5. Hire and Begin Work

Once you've found the right match:

1. Click "Hire" on the chosen proposal
2. Review and confirm the contract terms
3. Fund the project escrow (for fixed-price) or set up your payment method (for hourly)
4. Receive a notification when the freelancer accepts
5. Start collaborating through our platform

## For Freelancers: Finding and Winning Projects

### 1. Create Your Professional Profile

Your profile is your storefront on Shakers:

1. Sign up selecting "Join as a Freelancer"
2. Complete your profile thoroughly:
   - Professional headline and summary
   - Skills and expertise (with proficiency levels)
   - Experience and education
   - Portfolio items showcasing your best work
   - Profile photo and availability
   - Hourly rate or pricing structure

### 2. Take Skill Assessments

Verify your expertise to stand out:

1. Go to the "Skills" section of your profile
2. Select skills you want to verify
3. Complete the assessment tests
4. Showcase passed assessments on your profile

### 3. Find Suitable Projects

Discover projects that match your skills:

1. Browse the project marketplace
2. Use filters to find relevant opportunities:
   - Industry
   - Required skills
   - Project type
   - Budget range
3. Save projects you're interested in
4. Set up alerts for specific project types

### 4. Submit Compelling Proposals

Make your proposals stand out:

1. Carefully read the project description
2. Craft a personalized cover letter addressing:
   - Your understanding of the project
   - Relevant experience and skills
   - Your approach to completing the work
   - Expected timeline
3. Set a competitive but fair price
4. Highlight relevant portfolio items

### 5. Communicate Effectively

Once a client shows interest:

1. Respond promptly to messages
2. Be prepared for interviews and discussions
3. Ask clarifying questions about the project
4. Demonstrate your expertise and enthusiasm
5. Discuss terms and set clear expectations

### 6. Deliver Quality Work

After being hired:

1. Begin work according to the agreed timeline
2. Provide regular updates through the platform
3. Submit deliverables through the secure project space
4. Request feedback at key milestones
5. Address any revision requests promptly

## Using the Shakers Platform

### Dashboard Navigation

Your personalized dashboard provides easy access to:

- **Overview Tab**: Activity summary and key metrics
- **Projects Tab**: Current, pending, and completed projects
- **Messages**: Communication with clients/freelancers
- **Finances**: Payment history, invoices, and earnings/expenses
- **Profile**: Your public profile and settings

### Communication Tools

Stay connected through our integrated tools:

- Direct messaging system with file sharing
- Video conferencing for face-to-face meetings
- Collaborative document editing
- Real-time commenting on deliverables
- @mentions for drawing attention to specific items

### Payment Protection

Our secure payment system protects both parties:

- Escrow holding for fixed-price projects
- Verified hourly work tracking
- Milestone-based payments
- Dispute resolution process
- Secure payment processing

### Mobile Access

Stay productive on the go:

1. Download the Shakers app (iOS and Android)
2. Log in with your existing credentials
3. Receive real-time notifications
4. Respond to messages
5. Manage your projects from anywhere
