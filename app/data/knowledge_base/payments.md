# Payment System in Shakers

## Overview

Shakers provides a secure and transparent payment system to facilitate transactions between clients and freelancers. Our platform ensures that both parties are protected throughout the payment process.

## How Payments Work

1. **Project Creation**: When a client creates a project, they set a budget or hourly rate.

2. **Escrow System**: Once a freelancer is hired, the client funds the project by placing the agreed amount in escrow.

3. **Milestone Payments**: For fixed-price projects, clients can create milestones and release payments as deliverables are completed.

4. **Hourly Payments**: For hourly projects, freelancers log their hours using our time-tracking system, and clients are billed weekly.

5. **Payment Release**: Once the client approves the work, the funds are released to the freelancer's Shakers account.

6. **Withdrawal Options**: Freelancers can withdraw their earnings through various methods, including bank transfers, PayPal, and other payment services.

## Payment Protection

Shakers provides payment protection for both clients and freelancers:

- **For Clients**: Funds are only released to freelancers once you approve their work.
- **For Freelancers**: Clients deposit funds upfront, ensuring you'll be paid for completed work.

## Service Fees

Shakers charges the following fees:

- **Client Fee**: 5% of the project value
- **Freelancer Fee**: 10% of the earned amount

For enterprise clients with high volume, custom fee structures may be available.

## Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:

- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- PayPal
- Bank Transfers
- Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum)

## Invoicing

Freelancers can generate professional invoices directly from the platform. All invoices include:

- Project details
- Hourly breakdown (if applicable)
- Payment status
- Tax information

## Currency Support

Shakers supports transactions in multiple currencies, including:

- US Dollar (USD)
- Euro (EUR)
- British Pound (GBP)
- Canadian Dollar (CAD)
- Australian Dollar (AUD)
- Japanese Yen (JPY)

The platform automatically handles currency conversion using up-to-date exchange rates.

## Tax Information

Freelancers are responsible for their tax obligations. At the end of each fiscal year, Shakers provides:

- Annual earnings report
- Transaction history
- 1099 forms for US-based freelancers earning over $600

## Payment Disputes

In case of payment disputes:

1. First, we encourage direct communication between client and freelancer
2. If necessary, either party can open a dispute case
3. Our dispute resolution team will review the case
4. A decision will be made based on project terms, communication history, and submitted work

For more information about our payment system, please contact our support team.
