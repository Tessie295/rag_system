# Freelancers on Shakers

## What is a Freelancer?

A freelancer on Shakers is an independent professional who offers their services to clients on a project-by-project basis. Freelancers are not employees of either Shakers or the clients they work with but instead operate as independent contractors.

## Freelancer Profiles

Each freelancer on Shakers has a comprehensive profile that includes:

- Professional headline and summary
- Skills and expertise areas
- Portfolio of previous work
- Educational background
- Professional certifications
- Client reviews and ratings
- Hourly rate or project pricing
- Availability status

## Verification and Quality Control

Shakers verifies freelancer identities and qualifications through a multi-step process:

1. **Identity Verification**: Government ID and address verification
2. **Skills Assessment**: Optional skill tests to showcase proficiency
3. **Portfolio Review**: Quality check of submitted work samples
4. **Client Feedback**: Continuous monitoring of client reviews and ratings

## Hiring a Freelancer

To hire a freelancer on Shakers:

1. Browse freelancer profiles or post a project
2. Review qualifications, portfolios, and reviews
3. Interview potential candidates
4. Discuss project details and establish clear deliverables
5. Create a contract with payment terms
6. Fund the project escrow
7. Collaborate through our platform
8. Review and approve completed work
9. Release payment and provide feedback

## Freelancer Tiers

Shakers categorizes freelancers into experience tiers:

- **Rising Talent**: New to the platform but showing promise
- **Professional**: Established freelancers with a proven track record
- **Expert**: Top-rated professionals with exceptional skills and reviews
- **Enterprise**: Elite professionals qualified for enterprise-level projects

## Communication and Collaboration

Freelancers and clients collaborate through:

- Integrated messaging system
- Video conferencing
- File sharing and collaboration tools
- Project management features
- Time tracking (for hourly projects)
